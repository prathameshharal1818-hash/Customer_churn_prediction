About Customer Churn Prediction

Customer Churn Prediction is a Machine Learning project developed to predict whether a telecom customer is likely to leave the company based on customer information.

🎯 Objective

Predict customer churn to help telecom companies retain valuable customers.

🤖 Algorithm

Random Forest Classifier trained on customer telecom dataset.

💻 Technologies

Python, Flask, HTML, CSS, Scikit-Learn, Pandas, Joblib.

📊 Features Used

Tenure, Monthly Charges, Total Charges, Internet Service, Online Security, Contract Type, Payment Method and more.
